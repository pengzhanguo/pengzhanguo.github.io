About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science at Duke Kunshan University. My research focuses on machine learning, data mining, reinforcement learning, stochastic optimization, and parallel and distributed computing, with applications in intelligent decision-making, recommendation systems, transportation, and career planning.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Stony Brook University in 2021. Prior to that, I received my M.S. in Computational Applied Mathematics from Stony Brook University in 2018.

Research Interests

  • Machine learning and data mining
  • Reinforcement learning and multi-agent reinforcement learning
  • Stochastic optimization and large-scale optimization
  • Parallel and distributed machine learning
  • Intelligent recommendation and sequential decision-making
  • Applications in transportation, career planning, and urban computing
